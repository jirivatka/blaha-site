Privacy policy

Blaha has no backend. Your tasks go to the Vikunja server you chose and nowhere else, and nothing about you reaches us — because there is no "us" in the path.

Last updated 22 September 2026. If any of this stops being true, this page changes in the same release that changes it, not afterwards.

What we collect

Nothing. Blaha has no servers of its own, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see your tasks, your server's address, your name or your email, because none of it is ever sent to us.

The app's privacy manifest says the same thing in Apple's own terms: no tracking, no tracking domains, and an empty list of collected data types.

Where your data goes

Your Vikunja server
Tasks, projects, labels, comments and attachments are read from and written to the server address you enter — yours, or Vikunja Cloud's. That server is operated by you or by the provider you chose, under their terms, not ours.
Your device
A copy of what was last fetched is cached on the device so the app works offline, and changes made offline are queued there until they can be sent. Your API token or password is kept in the system Keychain. Widgets read the cached copy only; they never touch the token.
Apple
If you buy the unlock, Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details, name or email address — Blaha only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account owns the unlock.

Your calendar

Blaha shows calendar events beside your tasks. Those events are read from the calendars already on your device, through the system permission that you grant and can revoke at any time. They are never sent anywhere — not to your Vikunja server, not to us — and Blaha does not create or change events.

In the demo, the events are the demo's own sample data. No calendar permission is requested in demo mode and nothing of yours is read.

Notifications

Reminders and calendar alerts are scheduled by your own device. There is no push service, and no device token exists for anyone to hold — which is also why a reminder only fires while the app has been opened to schedule it.

Children

Blaha is a task manager for a server you run yourself. It is not directed at children.

Your rights

There is no account to close and no data of yours for us to hand over or erase, because we hold none. Deleting the app removes the cache, the queue and the Keychain entry from that device. The tasks themselves live on your Vikunja server and are yours to export or delete there.

If you want that confirmed in writing, or have any other request under the GDPR, write to info@redpixl.pictures.

This website

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. They use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views: it sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device, and cannot follow you to other sites. We see totals — which pages were viewed, roughly where from, which referrer — never individuals, and you can block it with any content blocker and the site works identically. We chose it over the usual alternatives precisely because it needs no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to when nothing is stored on your device. The app itself still collects nothing — no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs — and the app and the website are separate. Like any web host, GitHub processes the IP address of an incoming request in order to answer it; that is GitHub's own privacy statement, not ours. The support forum is on GitHub too, and anything posted there is public.

Contact

General questions about using the app are better in the support forum, where the answer helps the next person. ⚠️ The forum is public, so nothing private belongs there.
