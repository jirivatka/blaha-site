Forecast and Calendar

See the day as it really is.

Forecast puts the day's calendar events beside the tasks that are due, so an afternoon that is already full looks full. Month, week and day views read the calendars already on your device.

Tasks and defer dates Unlock

Only what you can do now.

Deferred work is hidden until its date, and the count of what is hidden is always shown. Blocked work waits in its own list, so the one in front of you is the one you can act on.

Projects and Review Unlock

Projects that cannot quietly go stale.

Nested projects, grouped in folders, with their available and deferred work separated. Each project gets a review cycle, and Blaha tells you when one is due.

Perspectives Unlock

Views of your own, one tap away.

Save a filter — next actions, this week, waiting on — and it takes a place in the sidebar, behaving like any other screen. On the iPad and the Mac, in the same three columns.

Timesheet Unlock

Know where the hours went.

Start a timer, or add time by hand, against any project. Set hourly rates, read hours by project and task, and export a CSV or a PDF to send with an invoice. A running timer stays in view on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on iPhone, in the menu bar on the Mac, and in a notification on Android.

See it in just over a minute.

What it costs

Free

A full task client: Forecast, Tasks, Calendar, Done, Tags, capture, the share extension and the widgets.

One-time unlock

Adds the seven that turn a list into a system: Projects, Blocked, Review, Perspectives, defer dates, multiple accounts and time tracking.

No subscription — a client for someone else's server has nothing honest to charge rent for. Everything is open for a 7-day trial from first launch, so the paid layer can be judged before it is bought.

What you need

  • iOS or iPadOS 17, or macOS 14, and later — or Android 8.0 and later.
  • A Vikunja server you host, or a Vikunja Cloud account, reachable over HTTPS.
  • Or neither: tap Try the demo on the first screen and Blaha runs against sample data held entirely on the device — no server, no account, no network.
